Apply for a Persist Foundation Scholarship

We reduce financial barriers to higher education so students can pursue their academic and professional goals without the burden of overwhelming debt.

$1,000 scholarship · Applications open March 1 – April 30, 2027

Before You Apply

Who should apply

Our scholarships are for young people who are pursuing higher education and who live out our motto, “Until I Succeed!” — students who keep going when the path gets hard, and who want to make a positive difference in their communities.

  • Students planning to attend, or currently attending, a college, university or other higher-education program
  • Students who have shown persistence through challenges in school or in life
  • Students involved in service, leadership or their community
